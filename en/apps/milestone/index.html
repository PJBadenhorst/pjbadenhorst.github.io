Milestone: Life Tracker

Track what matters. Build the life you want.

Milestone gives you a flexible set of tracking cards — each designed for a specific purpose — that you organise into Journeys, named workspaces you can switch between, share, or archive. Whether you're tracking how long you spend on a hobby, building a daily habit, counting down to a big event, or logging your mood each day, Milestone has a card for it. Everything is stored locally on your device, and privacy controls let you hide sensitive cards with a single tap.

Key Features:

  • Activity cards — start/stop timers to track time spent on any activity, with full session history and statistics
  • Date cards — count up from or down to any date, perfect for milestones, anniversaries, and deadlines
  • Achievement cards — daily habit checkboxes with streak tracking
  • Counter cards — simple increment counters with optional confirmation, validation rules, and history
  • Mood cards — log your mood on a 1–5 scale and track patterns over time
  • Journeys — group cards into named workspaces; shelve, share, or import them
  • Custom Views — a visual drag-and-drop editor to build your own display screens
  • Privacy mode — hide private cards with one tap; biometric lock support
  • Multilingual — English, Afrikaans, and Zulu supported

Availability:

Available on the Google Play Store for Android devices.
