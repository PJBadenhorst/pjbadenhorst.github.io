Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

In short

We keep personal information to a minimum.

  • Our apps store your data on your own device. We do not receive it.
  • This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read.
  • If you email us or join our testing programme, we keep the details you give us so we can reply and run the programme.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

Who we are

The Code Cottage ("we", "us", "our") is a software studio in South Africa that builds Android and web applications. We are the responsible party for the personal information described in this policy, as defined in the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). You can reach us at thecodecottage478@gmail.com.

Our apps

Our apps are designed to work on your device. For each app:

  • Milestone: Time Tracker stores your Journeys, cards and history locally on your device. There is no account, and we do not receive this data. If you use the privacy lock, your device's biometric check is handled by Android. We never see your fingerprint or face data.
  • Because the data stays on your device, it is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. If you back up your device, Android and Google may include the app's data in that backup under your own Google account settings.
  • Google Play may collect technical information such as crash reports and installation statistics when you download or use an app. That is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us.
  • If a future app or version collects or sends data, we will say so here and in the app's Play Store listing before it is released.

This website

When you visit this website, the following happens:

  • Google Analytics records technical and usage information such as the pages you view, how long you stay, your approximate location (country and city), and your device, browser and screen size.
  • Your browser stores a language preference so the site opens in your language. This stays in your browser and is not sent to us.
  • Our host, GitHub Pages, may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page. We do not have access to these logs.

Google Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, so we can improve it. It sets cookies in your browser, such as _ga, to tell visitors apart. We use this information only in aggregate and we do not try to identify individual visitors. We process it because we have a legitimate interest in improving the site. We keep analytics data for no longer than 14 months. You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works the same if you do.

Email and our testing programme

We collect personal information only when you give it to us:

  • If you email us, we keep your email address and message so that we can reply and, if needed, follow up.
  • If you apply to become a tester, give feedback, or ask to retire from testing, you do this through a Google Form. We receive the details you enter, for example your name and the email address linked to your Google Play account. Google processes the form under its own privacy policy.
  • We use tester details to invite you to test, send you tester emails about the status of the project, new builds and feedback requests, and respond to what you tell us.
  • When you retire from testing, we remove you from the tester list and stop sending tester emails. We delete your details unless the law requires us to keep them.

Who we share information with

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information. We share it only with the services we use to run the site and our programme, and only as needed:

  • Google: Google Analytics, Google Forms, Google Play and Gmail
  • GitHub: hosts this website
  • Authorities or other parties, only where the law requires it or to protect our rights and safety

Storage outside South Africa

Google and GitHub process information on servers in other countries, including the United States and Europe. By using the website or contacting us you accept that your information may be transferred there. Both companies state that they protect the information they hold.

How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as we need it. Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months. Emails are kept while we are dealing with your request, and then deleted or archived. Tester details are kept while you are a tester. If you want your information deleted sooner, contact us.

Security

We keep our accounts protected and limit who can see your information. We collect little personal information, and our apps keep data on your device rather than on our servers. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • Ask whether we hold personal information about you, and ask for a copy
  • Ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date or no longer needed
  • Object to us processing your information
  • Withdraw your consent where we rely on it, for example by retiring from the testing programme or asking us to stop emailing you
  • Complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za) if you believe we have not handled your information properly

Children

This website and our apps are not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

This site links to other services, including Google Play and Google Forms. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies before you share information with them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our apps or the law change. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will say so on our website or email testers.

Contact us

For questions, or to use any of your rights above, email thecodecottage478@gmail.com or use the details on our Contact page. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.
